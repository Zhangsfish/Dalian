# CR-S02｜Target Architecture and Content Map

目标：把当前 8 节中文经济学工作论文重排成适合 China Report 的英文 Research Article。

## A. Target article architecture

### 1. Introduction
回答四个问题：
- What is the puzzle?
- Why does Dalian matter?
- What is the argument?
- What evidence changes how we understand the case?

保留最少量数字：
- outside growth-gap reversal ≈ 4.6 pp;
- Dalian-vs-Liaoning deterioration ≈ 1.5 pp.

不在 Introduction 展开：
- TWFE 等价关系；
- SCM RMSPE；
- 全部 donor robustness。

### 2. Political Networks and Local Economic Vulnerability in China
合并当前：
- R02 political mechanisms
- R01 literature contribution
- 部分 regional-growth background

核心概念：
1. visible resource allocation;
2. marginal coordination/credit;
3. local structural vulnerability;
4. state-dependent effect of political shocks.

本节不是列 H1/H2/H3 的“经济学假说清单”，而是形成一个可读的政治经济学框架。

### 3. Dalian, the 2012 Shock, and Research Design
合并当前：
- institutional background;
- R03 data/sample/design;
- event definition.

必须解释：
- Bo Xilai–Dalian historical linkage;
- why 2012 is a meaningful network shock;
- why Wuhan/Qingdao;
- why Liaoning is a nested regional benchmark rather than a clean control;
- why Chongqing is a parallel exposed case.

方法写清楚，但不卖“准实验”。

### 4. Regional Slowdown and Dalian’s Relative Decline
合并当前：
- R04 baseline;
- R05.1 real estate / FAI.

叙事顺序：
1. Dalian clearly slows relative to outside cities;
2. most of the divergence appears at Liaoning/regional level;
3. real estate and fixed investment identify the regional growth regime that was turning.

这里第一次把“辽宁共同下行”说成人话：
> not Liaoning causing Dalian, but Dalian sharing a regional investment-industrial exposure.

### 5. From Industrial Cash Flow to Credit Contraction
合并当前：
- R05.2 sector decomposition;
- R05.3 credit/private investment.

这是全篇最强 mechanism section。

因果链：
regional investment slowdown
→ secondary-sector and construction weakness
→ revenue/profit deterioration
→ asset-quality deterioration
→ weaker bank credit / private investment
→ further investment retrenchment.

明确区分：
- evidence consistent with transmission;
- not a fully identified mediation design.

### 6. What Did Not Collapse: Public Resources and External Capital
合并当前：
- R05.4 fiscal/projects;
- R05.5 Japan.

功能：
- reject overly simple “Dalian lost all political resources” story;
- distinguish visible public resources from marginal finance;
- treat Japan as slow-moving structural exposure rather than 2014 trigger.

### 7. Chongqing and the Conditional Effect of Political Shocks
升级当前 R06，不再像 robustness appendix。

核心问题：
> If the political event itself mechanically produced macroeconomic collapse, why did Chongqing not follow the Dalian path?

展示：
- relative GDP path;
- credit;
- FAI / real estate.

结论：
same political shock + different growth regime → different macro trajectory.

### 8. Discussion and Limitations
合并当前 R07 + 原文散落的 caveats。

只集中讲一次：
- observational design;
- Liaoning contains Dalian;
- small donor pool;
- political-network channel is inferred rather than micro-identified;
- historical data revisions.

Discussion 要回答：
- what this case changes in the literature;
- when political ties matter more;
- distinction between access to formal public resources and marginal coordination.

### 9. Conclusion
控制在约 3–4 段。

不再逐条复述所有数字。
最后落到：
> political connections matter conditionally; macroeconomic consequences depend on the viability of the local investment and credit regime.

## D. Editorial logic test

A China Report editor should be able to summarize the paper in one sentence:

> The paper shows that Dalian’s post-2012 slowdown was rooted mainly in a regional investment and industrial downturn, while the loss of political-network value may have amplified adjustment through credit and coordination channels; Chongqing demonstrates that such political shocks have state-dependent economic effects.

If a section does not strengthen this sentence, it should be shortened, moved to supplement, or deleted.
