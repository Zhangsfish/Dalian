# China Report｜Final Figure and Table Captions

## Figure 1. Dalian's Relative Growth Slowdown after 2012

**Panel A** reports contemporaneous official real-GDP growth rates for Dalian, Wuhan and Qingdao. **Panel B** reports Dalian's annual growth-rate gap relative to the equal-weighted Wuhan-Qingdao benchmark and relative to Liaoning.

**Note:** Qingdao's 2012 growth value is excluded because the available source is a forecast rather than an observed annual result. Liaoning contains Dalian and is used as a nested regional benchmark, not an untreated control.

**Source:** Dalian, Wuhan, Qingdao and Liaoning annual statistical bulletins and official annual reports; underlying source IDs in the anonymized source ledger. Authors' calculations.

**Artwork:** `figures_cr/Figure1_relative_growth.eps`  
**Raw figure data:** `figure_data/Figure1_data.csv`

## Figure 2. The Regional Investment Regime Turns Down

**Panel A** reports annual real-estate development investment growth. **Panel B** indexes 2013 fixed-asset and real-estate investment levels to 100 for Dalian and Liaoning.

**Note:** The 2015 Liaoning real-estate growth rate is derived from reported investment levels. Investment definitions follow contemporaneous bulletins; detailed scope notes are provided in the supplement.

**Source:** Dalian, Liaoning, Wuhan and Qingdao statistical bulletins and government reports. Authors' calculations.

**Artwork:** `figures_cr/Figure2_regional_investment.eps`  
**Raw figure data:** `figure_data/Figure2_data.csv`

## Figure 3. Sectoral Slowdown and Industrial Earnings Stress in Dalian

**Panel A** reports Dalian's real-GDP, secondary-industry and tertiary-industry growth. **Panel B** reports growth in above-scale industrial main-business revenue, industrial profit and construction output.

**Note:** Revenue and profit are measures of industrial operating performance and are not direct firm cash-flow measures.

**Source:** Dalian annual statistical bulletins, 2013-2015.

**Artwork:** `figures_cr/Figure3_industrial_earnings.eps`  
**Raw figure data:** `figure_data/Figure3_data.csv`

## Figure 4. Bank-Credit Growth and Asset-Quality Deterioration

**Panel A** reports growth in outstanding bank loans for Dalian and Liaoning. **Panel B** reports Dalian's non-performing-loan ratio.

**Note:** Loan growth is derived from reported year-end balances and annual increments where official bulletins do not report the growth rate directly. Aggregate loan data do not distinguish credit-supply changes from borrowing-demand changes.

**Source:** Dalian and Liaoning statistical bulletins and People's Bank of China financial materials. Authors' calculations.

**Artwork:** `figures_cr/Figure4_credit_asset_quality.eps`  
**Raw figure data:** `figure_data/Figure4_data.csv`

---

## Figure 5. The 2012 Political Rupture and Different Local Trajectories: Dalian and Chongqing

**Panel A** reports Dalian's growth gap relative to Wuhan-Qingdao and Chongqing's growth gap relative to Chengdu-Wuhan. **Panel B** compares bank-loan growth. **Panel C** compares fixed-asset-investment growth.

**Note:** Chongqing is a parallel exposed case, not a matched or untreated control. The figure is used to assess whether the 2012 elite political rupture mechanically predicts a common macroeconomic trajectory across different local growth regimes.

**Source:** Dalian, Chongqing, Chengdu, Wuhan and Qingdao annual statistical bulletins and financial materials. Authors' calculations.

**Artwork:** `figures_cr/Figure5_dalian_chongqing.eps`  
**Raw figure data:** `figure_data/Figure5_data.csv`
