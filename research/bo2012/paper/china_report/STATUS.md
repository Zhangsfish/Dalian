## Main corrections

1. Political-network amplification and state dependence are hypotheses, not identified findings.
2. The same 2008–2011 linear pretrend procedure is now applied to all comparisons. The Dalian–Liaoning post-period trend residual is **+1.61 pp**, alongside the **−1.45 pp** mean-gap change. The external trend residual remains **−2.99 pp**. The old “1–2 pp political explanation space” claim is removed.
3. Chongqing uses the same 2013–2015 main window: relative mean **+2.15 pp**, change **+0.60 pp**. Both cases retain a separate 2016 extension.
4. Industrial revenue/profit and loan growth are not described as measured cash flow or a shrinking loan stock. Annual co-movement is not presented as identified causal ordering.
5. Fiscal/project continuity is not used to infer that an unmeasured political-credit channel must explain the slowdown.
6. Historical public availability and substantial AI involvement require truthful disclosure; unconfirmed statements of complete independent human verification are removed.
